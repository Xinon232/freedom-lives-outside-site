Freedom lives
outside.

A little pigeon. A timely nudge.
A reason to put your phone down.

FLO brings pixel-style reminders to your screen and small actions back into your day. Set the pace, choose which apps to exclude, and make room for life beyond the scroll.

Get help with FLOAbout lifetime access

A nudge, not a lock.

Choose your reminder intensity, pause when you need to, and keep selected apps out of the reminders. Android’s display-over-other-apps and usage-access settings make these reminders possible. You stay in control of those permissions.

Make the next tap count.

Pigeon Link can take you from a reminder to a small real-world action. English and Spanish are supported in the app.
